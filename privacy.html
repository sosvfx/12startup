Privacy Policy

Your privacy comes first.

Simple. Private. Local.

12StartUp is designed with privacy in mind.

The application works without user accounts and does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Whenever possible, your data stays entirely on your own device.

Personal Information

12StartUp does not collect or process personally identifiable information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages
  • Files

No personal information is transmitted to the developer.

Local Storage

To provide its functionality, the application stores certain information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your goals
  • Your progress
  • Virtual trees and application content
  • Application settings
  • Selected language
  • Notification preferences
  • Premium subscription status

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

Notifications

12StartUp uses local notifications that you choose to enable.

Notifications are created and scheduled directly on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Premium Subscription

Premium features are available through a monthly subscription using Google Play Billing.

All payment processing is handled securely by Google. 12StartUp never receives or stores your payment card information.

Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

12StartUp uses Google Play Billing solely for subscription management.

The application does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • User tracking
  • Crash reporting services
  • Behavior profiling

Children's Privacy

Because 12StartUp does not collect personal information, no personal information from children is knowingly collected.

Your Data

Your data belongs to you.

You may remove all locally stored information at any time by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
