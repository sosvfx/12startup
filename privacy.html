Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Introduction

Thank you for using 12StartUp. 12StartUp is designed with privacy in mind. The application works without user accounts and keeps your data on your own device whenever possible. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information and protects your privacy.

Personal Information

12StartUp does not require account registration and does not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages

All your personal data remains under your control and is never sent to the developer.

Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its functionality, 12StartUp stores certain application data locally on your device. This may include:

  • Your goals
  • Your progress
  • Trees and application content
  • Application settings
  • Selected language
  • Notification preferences
  • Subscription status

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

Notifications

12StartUp uses local notifications that you choose to enable. These notifications are generated entirely on your own device. No notification data is sent to the developer.

Premium Subscription

Premium features are available through a monthly subscription using Google Play Billing.

All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play. The application does not receive or store your payment card information.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Third-Party Services

12StartUp uses Google Play Billing solely for processing premium subscriptions.

The application does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking technologies.

Children's Privacy

12StartUp does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Your information is stored locally on your own device. You may remove all stored data at any time by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
