Privacy Policy

Your privacy comes first.

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Simple. Private. Yours.

12StartUp is designed with privacy in mind.

The application works without user accounts and does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. All application data is stored locally on your own device.

Personal Information

12StartUp does not collect or process personally identifiable information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages
  • Files

No personal information is transmitted to the developer. The application can be used without creating an account or signing in.

Local Storage

To provide its functionality, the application stores certain information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your goals
  • Your progress
  • Virtual trees and application content
  • Application settings
  • Selected language
  • Notification preferences
  • Premium subscription status

This information is stored only to provide the application's core functionality. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

Notifications

12StartUp uses local notifications that you choose to enable.

Notifications are created and scheduled directly on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Premium Subscription

Premium features are available through a monthly subscription using Google Play Billing Library.

Subscription purchases are processed by Google Play. 12StartUp does not have access to your payment details.

Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

12StartUp uses Google Play Billing solely for subscription management.

The application does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • User tracking
  • Crash reporting services
  • Behavior profiling

Children's Privacy

Because 12StartUp does not collect personal information, no personal information from children is knowingly collected.

Your Data

Your data belongs to you.

You can delete all locally stored application data at any time by:

  • clearing the application's storage in your device settings;
  • or uninstalling the application.

Permissions

12StartUp requests only the permissions required for its core functionality.

For example:

  • Notification permission — used only to schedule reminders that you choose to enable.

No permission is used to collect personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when changes were made.
